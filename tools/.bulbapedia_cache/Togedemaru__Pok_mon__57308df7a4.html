Togedemaru (Japanese: トゲデマル Togedemaru) is a dual-type Electric/Steel Pokémon introduced in Generation VII.

It is not known to evolve into or from any other Pokémon.

Biology

Togedemaru is a spherical, rodent-like Pokémon. Most of its front is white, while its back is primarily gray. It has black eyes, a short pointed snout with a black nose, rounded ears, and a circular, yellow marking on each cheek. Scattered across its back are 14 fur spikes that appear as yellow and brown triangular markings. When attacking, surprised, or agitated, these spikes will stand straight up. There is a tail-like needle on the back of its head. The needle is gray with a yellow tip and is shaped like a lightning bolt. Additionally, it has stubby arms and legs. When Togedemaru's spines are out, its cheek marks sometimes look like electrical outlets.

Togedemaru uses the needle on top of its head to attract and gather electricity. Groups can be seen gathered with their needles standing upright, waiting to be struck by lightning on stormy days to make up for its somewhat limited electric capacity. Some Trainers use its ability to absorb electricity as a strategy in battle. The opponent is coaxed into unleashing an Electric-type attack, and then has that power turned against it.[1] When it is pleased, this Pokémon will curl into a ball and roll around uncontrollably. Togedemaru clashes with Elekid whenever it tries to steal the latter's electricity, usually ending with even matches. As mentioned in Sleep Style Dex, Togedemaru usually sleeps while curled up with its spiny fur on its back at rest. Despite being asleep, one can see its fur stand on end for protection if the Pokémon should become agitated.

A special Togedemaru in Alola acts as the Totem Pokémon for Sophocles's trial in Hokulani Observatory. This Togedemaru possesses Z-Power, an energy that originates from Ultra Wormholes and takes the form of a golden, flaring aura. The reward for defeating Totem Togedemaru and completing the trial is an Electrium Z.

In Generation VII, Zing Zap was its signature move.

NPC appearances

  • In Pokémon Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon, a Togedemaru nicknamed Togemaru resides at the Hokulani Observatory on Ula'ula Island. During the player's trial, Togemaru uses its Lightning Rod Ability to protect Sophocles's Charjabug from a blast of electricity.

Super Smash Bros. Ultimate

SmashWiki has more about this subject:
Togedemaru

Togedemaru appears as a Poké Ball summon. It uses Spark, calling down a lightning bolt on itself in order to shoot out sparks from its body, before bouncing to another location on the stage to repeat its behavior. Togedemaru also appears as a Shield Type Primary Spirit of novice ranking that provides a boost in electric-themed moves. It is the 554th Spirit It can be obtained either in the Vault Shop, or through the Spirit Board and battled.

In a spirit battle, Togedemaru is represented as four Pichu who favor using Thunder on Prism Tower. Poké Balls only containing Togedemaru appear, as well as Uniras.

  • Togedemaru was featured as a glowing toy in the Jollibee Pokémon toys.

Origin

Togedemaru appears to be based on a hedgehog, due to its round body and ability to curl up into a spiky ball. It may also be based on a New World porcupine, given how it can stiffen its triangular body spines. Additionally, it bears some resemblance to a chinchilla or a Ryūkyū spiny rat.

According to Togedemaru's Pokédex entry, the tail-like spike on its back acts as a lightning rod. When Togedemaru's spines come out, marks may appear on its cheeks that resemble North American and Japanese electrical outlets.

Name origin

Togedemaru may be a combination of 棘 toge (thorn, spike) or 棘鼠 togenezumi (Ryūkyū spiny rats), 電気 denki (electricity), and 丸 maru (round), as well as a play on 棘で丸 toge de maru ("spiny and round").

Gouging Fire (Japanese: ウガツホムラ Ugatsuhomura) is a dual-type Fire/Dragon Paradox Pokémon introduced during Generation IX, in The Indigo Disk.

While it is not known to evolve into or from any other Pokémon, Gouging Fire is said to be either an ancient relative of Entei or a version of it from a parallel timeline.

Biology

Gouging Fire is a massive lion ceratopsian hybrid Pokémon with some mastiff qualities. It is covered in brown fur with three green spike lines on both back legs. There is cream belly fur on its chest. It has five grey spikes on its back and a long, light gray cloud of smoke running along its entire back, resembling a tail. Gouging Fire has four gray paws. The two front paws house three small, red claws. The paws are covered with brown pads and two black cuff-like bands on its front legs and the back legs have only one. Its main faceplate is red and shaped like fins or a four-sided star with a golden ceratopsian-shaped skull headpiece. Behind the faceplate is two long brown fur with gold ends on each side of the hair. The headpiece has five gold and yellow horns on each side. At the center is two green circles with two smaller red circles inside. A gray plate, resembling a horseshoe mustache, covers its muzzle. It has shorter green fur around its blue eyes.

Gouging Fire is noteworthy for the large, spiky crest on its head, and is believed to have a powerful control over fire. There are scant few reports of this creature being sighted, though one short video shows it rampaging and spouting pillars of flame.[1] It resembles an eerie Pokémon once shown once in Occulture, and it was said to be an Entei regenerated from a fossil. It is rumored that Gouging Fire can summon magma eruptions simply by roaring.[2] Gouging Fire can superheat its fur to both protect itself and burn any foes that make physical contact with it.

According to AI Sada, Gouging Fire is an ancestor of Entei from the ancient past. However, according to a version of Professor Sada that was summoned at the Crystal Pool, Gouging Fire is a counterpart of Entei from another timeline. Its true origin is unknown. Gouging Fire was brought to the depths of Area Zero by a time machine built by Professor Sada ten years ago. 200 years ago, Heath also encountered Gouging Fire, as seen in the Scarlet Book, though it is unclear how it arrived there at the time.

Gouging Fire is the only known Pokémon capable of learning the move Burning Bulwark.

Main series

Major appearances

Lucius's Gouging Fire

Gouging Fire debuted in The Pillar of Fire. It is one of the Six Heroes, the group of Pokémon that once belonged to the Ancient Adventurer, Lucius. Due to the mysteries surrounding Paradox Pokémon, it was previously thought to be an Entei. In the same episode, it returned to its Ancient Poké Ball and allowed itself to be under Liko's care. In Battle for the Six Heroes!, it Terastallized into a Rock type due to Pagogo's Terastal energy.

Trivia

  • Before the official reveal of Gouging Fire, a sketch of an imaginary Pokémon similar to it appeared in the Scarlet Book in Pokémon Scarlet as an amalgam of the Legendary beasts (Raikou, Entei, and Suicune).
  • Gouging Fire can be considered the counterpart of Iron Boulder as both were not revealed until The Indigo Disk was released.
  • Gouging Fire is the heaviest Paradox Pokémon overall.
  • Gouging Fire is the only Pokémon whose weight in kilograms is the same as its base stat total.
  • Gouging Fire is the only Paradox Pokémon whose signature move is a status move.

Concept and development

Gouging Fire is based on Entei, which was designed by Muneo Saitō.

Origin

Gouging Fire is based on Entei. It also resembles ceratopsian dinosaurs, most notably the Styracosaurus and Sinoceratops due to both having a similar arrangement of horns around the head. Conceptually speaking, it could be inspired by the Deccan Traps, a volcanic formation in India whose activity is theorized to have had a great influence on the extinction of dinosaurs. The Deccan Traps started being formed in the Cretaceous period, the era in which ceratopsians lived. Gouging Fire's resemblance to Entei would therefore come from Entei being associated with volcanoes.

Name origin

Gouging Fire is literally gouging fire.

Ugatsuhomura can be taken literally as 穿つ炎 ugatsu homura (piercing flame).

Popplio (Japanese: アシマリ Ashimari) is a Water-type Pokémon introduced in Generation VII.

It evolves into Brionne starting at level 17, which evolves into Primarina starting at level 34.

Along with Rowlet and Litten, Popplio is one of the three first partner Pokémon of Alola available at the beginning of Pokémon Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun, and Ultra Moon. They were also the second Generation VII Pokémon to be revealed to the public on May 10, 2016, after Magearna.

Biology

Popplio is a pinniped Pokémon that is primarily blue. It has large eyes, a long, white snout with black whiskers, and a round, pink nose. There is a small, rounded earflap on each side of its head. Around its neck is a light blue ruff, which extends past its shoulders. It has four flippers. The front flippers are larger than the hind and have white markings separating its toes.

Popplio has complete control of its water bubbles, which it snorts from its nose and uses as part of its battle strategy. On land, it uses the elasticity of its bubbles to perform acrobatic stunts and jumps. Popplio practices daily with increasingly larger water bubbles, with the sheer force of them bursting dealing a lot of damage. It can swim at 25 mph (40 km/h).[1] It is famous for its hard-working nature. It has been recently seen living in the Terarium of Blueberry Academy.

Popplio and its evolved forms are the only known Pokémon that can have Liquid Voice as an Ability.

Trivia

  • Popplio's Japanese name and its romanization were revealed in a tweet of their trademark on April 17, 2016.
  • Popplio shares its category with Seel and Dewgong. They are all known as the Sea Lion Pokémon.
  • Popplio and its evolved forms are the only first partner Pokémon to have a piscine body.
  • Along with Snivy and Litten, Popplio is currently one of the three pre-Generation VIII first partner Pokémon that cannot be transferred to any Pokémon Legends game.

Merchandise

  • Popplio was featured as a spinning collar toy in the Jollibee Pokémon toys.
  • Popplio was featured as a water-drawing/shooting toy in the 2017 McDonald's promotional Pokémon toys.
  • Popplio was featured as a TCG card in the 2021 McDonald's promotional Pokémon toys.

Origin

Popplio may be based on a sea lion and a Hawaiian monk seal with elements of a circus clown. Popplio's propensity for blowing bubbles from its nose may also be based on common circus trick where sea lions balance balls on their noses or on the hooded seal and its inflatable nasal cavity.

When it is viewed standing up from the front with its head pointed up, it resembles the alchemical symbol for mercury, which represented water in alchemy. This is to complement Litten's forehead resembling the symbol for sulfur and Rowlet resembling the symbol for salt.

Name origin

Popplio may be a combination of sea lion pup, pop, pōpō (Hawaiian for ball), and ʻīlio-holo-i-ka-uaua (Hawaiian for the Hawaiian monk seal; meaning "dog that runs in rough water").

Ashimari may be a combination of 海驢 ashika (sea lion), 鞠 mari (ball), and marine.

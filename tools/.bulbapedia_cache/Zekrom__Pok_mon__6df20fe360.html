Zekrom (Japanese: ゼクロム Zekrom) is a dual-type Dragon/Electric Legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation V.

It is not known to evolve into or from any other Pokémon.

Zekrom, Reshiram, and Kyurem are often collectively referred to as the Tao trio by fans. The group is referred to in the myths and legends of the Unova region.

Zekrom is the game mascot of Pokémon White Version, appearing on its boxart.

Zekrom can absofuse with Kyurem using the DNA Splicers to become Black Kyurem.

Biology

Zekrom is a black, bipedal Pokémon resembling a saurian winged dragon, with a primarily charcoal-colored hide and pure black patches on various portions of its body. It has red sclerae, white irises, and black pupils, as well as a small horn-like protrusion on the tip of its snout. Its head sports a thundercloud-like crest tipped with light-blue coloration. There are six pairs of pointed teeth visible when its mouth is open, two in the upper jaw and four in the lower. At the base of its neck is a black, stud-like feature, and atop its shoulders are similar, larger features resembling armor padding. The bases of its wings also have such padding and a fanned, splayed, seemingly webbed shape with a small "wing-spike" on one edge. The lower lengths of Zekrom's arms extend into splayed, seemingly webbed, somewhat baseball-mitt-like formations, with three-clawed, dark-colored hands on the undersides. The front portion of Zekrom's waist is black-colored, with its belly and thighs having thin striations down their length. Its knees are black, as are the foremost parts of its three-clawed feet and spiked heels. Its tail consists of a large, round, conical, and spiked formation, with a black inner portion and bands on the tip of the outside portion. When its emotions surge, Zekrom activates Overdrive mode, and its tail lights up with a blue light.

In Unovan legends, Zekrom and Reshiram were created from a single dragon Pokémon commanded by the twin heroes of Truth and Ideals when it split into two. Zekrom assists anyone who wants to create an ideal world, so it allied itself with the younger brother, whereas Reshiram sided with the older brother, who sought the truth. The two dragon Pokémon battled each other, but were equally matched and unable to achieve victory, so the brothers ceased their conflict agreed that neither of them were right. However, their sons inherited their ideologies and resumed their clash; the Unova region was subsequently devastated by the combined might of the lightning and fire powers of Zekrom and Reshiram before the two Pokémon disappeared. Zekrom will transform into the Dark Stone should its body get destroyed or when attacked by Kyurem's tendril-like appendages. It can willingly revert back to normal when awakened by a hero who believes in ideals.

Zekrom flies throughout Unova while concealing itself in thunderclouds. It has a large generator in its tail that it uses to create electricity, which is powerful enough to scorch the world. Zekrom and Reshiram activate each others' tail generators when they use an enormous thunderbolt and an enormous flame, respectively, at the same time, powering up both attacks. When the inside of its tail spins in a motor-like fashion, it blasts its surroundings with several lightning blasts. It is said in mythology that Zekrom annihilates the kingdoms of people who have lost the righteousness in their hearts with its lightning. In Pokémon the Movie: Black - Victini and Reshiram and White - Victini and Zekrom, Zekrom is shown to be able to speak through telepathy. It loves Zekrom Treats made by Snacksworth, who once encountered it in the past.

Zekrom is the only known Pokémon capable of learning the move Bolt Strike by leveling up. Additionally, it and Black Kyurem are the only known Pokémon capable of learning the move Fusion Bolt by leveling up. They are also the only known Pokémon that can have Teravolt as an Ability.

Game data

NPC appearances

Spoiler warning: this article may contain major plot or ending details.

In the core series

  • Pokémon Black: During the game's plot, N awakens and catches Zekrom at the Dragonspiral Tower. It is used in battle against the player during the climax.
  • Pokémon White: During the game's climax, the player awakens Zekrom at N's Castle. After being caught, it can be added to the party immediately to battle N and his Reshiram.
  • Pokémon Black 2: N's Zekrom once again appears during the climax at Giant Chasm, being forcibly absofused with Kyurem to create Black Kyurem, being separated after Black Kyurem's defeat. The player can battle against N and his Zekrom during the post game, after which N will return it to the Dark Stone, allowing the player to catch it at Dragonspiral Tower.

In the spin-off games

  • PokéPark 2: Wonders Beyond: Zekrom is a Pokémon that watches over PokéPark alongside Reshiram. Together, they task Pikachu and his Pals to save PokéPark from a looming threat. Zekrom resides in a domain that can be accessed from the dais located in Wish Palace, if the player has broken the Deep Black Orb and obtained the Lightning Spike inside it.

In animation

Main series

Major appearances

Zekrom (M14)

Zekrom appeared in White—Victini and Zekrom and Black—Victini and Reshiram. In the former, it is controlled by Ash, while in the latter, it is controlled by Damon. Along with Reshiram, Zekrom can speak through telepathy.

Zekrom (recurring)

Zekrom debuted in the opening sequence of In The Shadow of Zekrom!. It made its full appearance later on, where it appeared above the skies of Nuvema Town. Its huge lightning bolt zapped Pikachu, making him unable to use his electricity. It reappeared in Enter Iris and Axew!, where Pikachu recovered his electric powers after being zapped again by another of Zekrom's lightning bolts.

Zekrom made further appearances in flashbacks throughout Pokémon the Series: Black & White.

Its only known move is Thunder.

Minor appearances

A Zekrom appeared in a fantasy in Farewell, Unova! Setting Sail for New Adventures!.

Super Smash Bros. for Nintendo 3DS

Zekrom appears on the Unova Pokémon League stage, where it will cover itself in a ball of lightning and hit the stage. Any fighters it touches will be damaged and if it hits the side of the stage, the stage will be tipped. It will also destroy the steps attached to the stage if there are any.

Zekrom also appears as a trophy in the 3DS game.

Trophy information

Zekrom's jet-black body contrasts sharply with the pure white of Reshiram, but the two do have some similarities: both are Legendary Pokémon said to have world-destroying powers. Zekrom's tail acts as a generator that can scorch the world with lightning, but it is said to assist those who wish to build an ideal world.

Trivia

  • Zekrom was first revealed simultaneously alongside Reshiram on both the official English and Japanese Pokémon websites on May 28, 2010. This makes them the first two Pokémon simultaneously revealed worldwide.
  • On May 31, 2010, two weeks prior to the official revelation of Zekrom's type, Shōko Nakagawa spoke about her love of Electric-type Pokémon on her blog and mentioned that because of that, she wanted to get a Zekrom. This statement was then quickly removed.[citation needed]
  • In games prior to Pokémon Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire, Zekrom has a catch rate of 45. This is likely due to it being a required capture during the storyline of Pokémon Black and White.
  • Zekrom is the heaviest Pokémon introduced in Generation V.
  • In the Generation V games, Zekrom, Reshiram, and Kyurem use different variations of the same battle theme. In Pokémon Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire and Pokémon Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon, however, Zekrom's version (featuring electric sound effects) is used for all three Pokémon.
    • This is not the case for Pokémon Scarlet and Violet, however, as those games do have each of the trio using their own variants of their battle themes.
  • Zekrom and Reshiram, from the Black & White set, were the first Pokémon to be featured on a Full Art card in the Pokémon Trading Card Game.
  • Zekrom is Ken Sugimori's favorite Pokémon from Pokémon Black and White.[1]

Concept and development

The initial design drafts for what would become Zekrom and its counterpart Reshiram were done by Keiko Moritsugu,[2] while their final designs were created by Ken Sugimori.[3]

According to Moritsugu, Zekrom's final design is mostly similar to her draft, while Reshiram's final design ended up being significantly different from her draft.[2] According to Junichi Masuda, Zekrom's design was originally supposed to have four hands.[4]

Cultural impact

Zekrom, as well as Reshiram, made an appearance alongside Pikachu and a Poké Ball in 2010's Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade.

Origin

Zekrom and its counterpart, Reshiram, appear to be based on the concept of yin and yang in Taoism, directly referenced by Zekrom's Japanese category name: こくいんポケモン Black Yin Pokémon. Zekrom's design was made to be tough and masculine, to contrast Reshiram's light, airy, graceful, and feminine design.[3][4] Its masculine design, however, contrasts the traditionally feminine characterization of yin in Taoism. Its tail, the center point of its design, is based on an electric turbine.[3]

Zekrom in its Dark Stone form may take inspiration from the Jewish concepts of Urim and Thummim (possibly translated as “light and perfection”). In pop culture, they are commonly depicted as a white and black stone. Its association with ideals would align with the idea of perfection.

Appearance-wise, it appears to be based on a European dragon and somewhat resembles a mid-sized to large theropod dinosaur, such as Allosaurus or Tyrannosaurus. The crest on its head seems to be based on a thundercloud; likewise, its black coloration seems to be a reference to such clouds as well as the darkened skies during thunderstorms. The mythology about how Zekrom and Reshiram came about, regarding two brothers infighting, also bears some similarities to Romulus and Remus or Cain and Abel. There is also some resemblance to the Zoroastrian belief of two equally powerful deities, one representing light and truth, and the other representing lies and darkness. In this case, Zekrom can be taken to resemble Angra Mainyu, as opposed to Reshiram resembling Ahura Mazda, although Zekrom is not evil; rather it is an equally powerful opposing Legendary Pokémon. Its association with lightning may be a reference to any number of dragons in contemporary media that possess electrical powers, such as lightning breath or electrokinesis.

Name origin

Zekrom is derived from 黒 kuro (black). Ken Sugimori has also stated that Ze was used because it was a harder, stronger sound than Reshiram's Re, as Zekrom is a "powerful, strong character."[5]

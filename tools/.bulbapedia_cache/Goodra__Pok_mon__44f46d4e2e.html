Goodra (Japanese: ヌメルゴン Numelgon) is a Dragon-type Powerhouse Pokémon introduced in Generation VI.

It evolves from Sliggoo starting at level 50 when leveled up during rain in the overworld. It is the final form of Goomy.

In Hisui, Goodra has a dual-type Steel/Dragon regional form, Hisuian Goodra. It evolves from Hisuian Sliggoo starting at level 50 during rain in the overworld. It is the final form of Goomy in the Hisui region.

(Specifics may differ in past games. Refer to Game data→Evolution data for these details.)

Biology

Goodra is a bipedal, draconic Pokémon that is coated with slime. It has a plump abdomen, and its body is pale purple with a darker purple underside. It has bright green eyes, two green spots on each cheek, a relatively long neck, and a short, rounded snout. On top of its head are two pairs of antenna-like horns. The foremost horns are small nubs, but the hind horns are longer, thicker, and curve down its neck. The tips of the larger horns are slightly curled. It has proportionally short limbs with two digits each and a large, thick tail with a curled tip. Along each side of its tail are three green spots. A pale green slime is usually visible dripping from its chin and arms. It is known for having a great affinity for water, being able to live within water with no difficulty at all, as well as dive and surf all around the area.

Because of its meek, friendly nature, Goodra is often teased for supposed weakness. When it grows close to someone, it will hug them and leave them coated in slime. If it is left alone, it will ooze gooey tears. Goodra usually misunderstands instructions, appearing dazed or bewildered. Despite this, many Trainers don't mind it, instead finding this behavior to be adorable. However, it will strike at its tormentors with its retractable horns and powerful tail. Despite appearing docile, Goodra can become uncontrollable once enraged. The horns can hit 100 times harder than the blow of a heavyweight boxer. It is also capable of throwing punches with incredible force. Its slimy coating in combination with a layer of fat causes attacks to slide off. Goodra lives in plains and mountains, usually during rainy days.

Prior to Generation IX, Goodra and its pre-evolved forms were the only known Pokémon that could have Gooey as an Ability.

Forms

Goodra has an ancient regional form: Hisuian Goodra.

Hisuian Goodra has droopy, gray eyes as well as a thinner body and legs. Its horns are longer and thinner than Kalosian Goodra's horns. It sports a large, spiral-shaped metallic shell on its back, which it can tuck its body into to protect itself, leaving only its head outside of the shell to observe its surroundings. It can control the hardness of its shell at will. It has fewer gray spots than Kalosian Goodra, with one on each side of its cheek and two on each side of its shell. From its chin and arms drips a metallic gray slime.

Hisuian Goodra has a clingy disposition and hates being alone; it will become angry and run riot if its closest companions leave its side. If anything bad happens to Hisuian Goodra, it will retreat into its shell and bounce away like a rubber ball. While in this state, Hisuian Goodra is powerful enough to knock down trees and roll into mountains. It has been recently seen inside Hyperspace Lumiose.

Hisuian Goodra and its pre-evolved forms are the only known Pokémon capable of learning the move Shelter.

Evolution

Goodra evolves from Sliggoo and is the final form of Goomy.

In Hisui, Goodra has a regional form that evolves from Hisuian Sliggoo and is the final form of Goomy in the Hisui region.

Evolution data

Goodra evolves from Sliggoo starting at level 50 when leveled up during rain in the overworld. In Generation VII only, Sliggoo will also evolve when leveled up during fog in the overworld. Rain instigated by Rain Dance, Drizzle, or Primordial Sea will not trigger Sliggoo's Evolution. It is the final form of Goomy.

In Hisui, Goodra has a dual-type Steel/Dragon regional form. It evolves from Hisuian Sliggoo starting at level 50 during rain in the overworld. It is the final form of Goomy in the Hisui region. All Goomy in Hisui will evolve into Hisuian Sliggoo regardless of their game of origin.

Trivia

  • Kalosian Goodra shares its category with the Horsea and Dratini evolutionary lines, along with Salamence. They are all known as the Dragon Pokémon.
  • In Pokémon Legends: Arceus's internal code, Hisuian Goodra's secondary Ability is Overcoat instead of Shell Armor.
  • Goodra is currently the only Pokémon who evolves from its pre-evolved form, Sliggoo, solely during a specific weather condition, that being rain.
  • Goodra is the only Powerhouse Pokémon:
    • to have an Evolution method requiring more than just leveling up.
    • to have a regional form.
  • Kalosian Goodra is the only Powerhouse Pokémon to only have one type, not counting Mega Garchomp Z.
  • Goodra and Hydreigon are the only Powerhouse Pokémon with a higher Special Attack stat than Physical.
    • Goodra has the lowest Attack stat of all Powerhouse Pokémon.
  • Goodra is the first Powerhouse Pokémon to not be immune to any types through ordinary type matchups. However, if its Ability is Sap Sipper, that would give it immunity to Grass-type moves.
  • Goodra's 150 base Special Defense stat is the highest single base stat of any non–Mega-Evolved Powerhouse Pokémon.
  • Hisuian Goodra is tied with Registeel and Probopass for the highest base Special Defense stat of all Steel-type Pokémon.
  • Every one of Kalosian Goodra's base stats are exactly double those of Goomy, with Kalosian Sliggoo's being halfway in between (rounded up).

Concept and development

Kalosian Goodra and its unevolved form, Goomy, were designed by Saya Tsuruta.[1]

Origin

Goodra, due to its Dragon type, elongated body shape, and retractable tentacles used for battling, is likely based on Lou Carcolh, a mythical serpent/mollusk creature from French folklore.

Like its pre-evolved forms, it could also be based on soft-bodied gastropods, such as slugs and nudibranch; in particular, the "blue dragon" sea slug. Goodra's Pokédex entry may be a reference to slug's retractable eyes, which retract when they are in danger.

Alternatively, it could be based on the yōkai called Shussebora, meaning "ascended trumpet shell", which is a trumpet shell snail that lives 1000 years in the mountains, 1000 on the plains, and 1000 at sea, then will ascend and turn into a dragon.

Hisuian Goodra is likely based on a snail. Its metallic shell in particular may be based on the scaly-foot gastropod, a species of deep-sea snail unique for its shell, which consists of iron sulfide compounds. The brown color of its Shiny variant's shell may reference how metal rusts when exposed to water for too long. Its Pokédex entry may be a reference to mercury. Mercury has historically been used to treat various disorders including melancholy in ancient Greece and Medieval Europe[2]. However, it is actually a deadly neurotoxin which with repeated exposure will cause various physical and mental disorders overtime. While Sliggoo's entry mentions melancholy implying mild exposure, Goodra appears to have progressed to full mood disorder. Alternatively, this mood could reflect a pun with the Japanese word 湿っぽい (shimeppoi), which can mean "gloomy, melancholic", but also "humid, damp", as a reference to the evolutionary line's usual habitat.

Name origin

Goodra may be a combination of goo and dragon or Hydra. It may also be an inversion of Dragoon. It may also derive from good, referring to its kindly nature as opposed to the more violent dispositions of other Powerhouse Pokémon.

Numelgon may be a combination of 滑る numeru (to be slippery), gel, and dragon.

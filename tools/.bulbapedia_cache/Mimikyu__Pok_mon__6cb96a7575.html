Mimikyu (Japanese: ミミッキュ Mimikkyu) is a dual-type Ghost/Fairy Pokémon introduced in Generation VII.

While it is not known to evolve into or from any other Pokémon, Mimikyu changes from its Disguised Form into its Busted Form when it is hit by an move.

Biology

Mimikyu is a small Pokémon whose true body is almost entirely concealed beneath an old rag. At only 0.2 m (8 in) tall, it is one of the shortest Ghost-type Pokémon, though its disguise increases its apparent height to approximately 0.56 m (1 ft 10 in).[1][2] Mimikyu's Pokédex data records its height without its disguise. Beneath the hem of its costume, a fringe of its amorphous lower body is visible. Mimikyu's eyes can be seen through holes in the disguise as beady black dots, though the animated series depicts them as purple. When angered, its eyes glow yellow. It can also extend a long, black, claw-like appendage from beneath the rag. The disguise itself resembles a Pikachu, consisting of a yellow cloth with black-tipped ears. Its face appears to be crudely drawn with crayons, featuring black eyes, red cheeks, and a squiggly black mouth. Mimikyu carries a small stick shaped like a lightning bolt to imitate Pikachu's tail. The head of the costume is hollow and can be used to store objects.

A special Mimikyu in Alola acts as the Totem Pokémon for Acerola's trial in an abandoned Thrifty Megamart. This Mimikyu possesses Z-Power, an energy that originates from Ultra Wormholes and takes the form of a golden, flaring aura. The reward for defeating Totem Mimikyu and completing the trial is a Ghostium Z.

Mimikyu is the only known Pokémon capable of using the Z-Move Let's Snuggle Forever. It is also the only known Pokémon that can have Disguise as an Ability.

Forms

Mimikyu has two forms: Disguised Form and Busted Form.

Disguised Form

In its Disguised Form, Mimikyu's costume remains intact and maintains its Pikachu-like appearance. The rag stands upright, concealing Mimikyu's true form from view. Only its eyes, the lower fringe of its body, and occasionally its claw-like appendage are visible beneath the disguise.

Mimikyu is a silent, lonely Pokémon who disguises itself as a Pikachu in the hopes of making friends with people and Pokémon because of the rising popularity of Pikachu-styled merchandise from two decades ago.[3] Despite its attempts to befriend others by appearing harmless, its cloth unfortunately only ends up making it look creepier. Its relationship with Pikachu can vary between individuals and canons, with some being antagonistic, as seen in Pokémon the Series: Sun & Moon, or friendly, as shown in the A Mimikyu Bedtime Story short. Its height in its Disguised Form is similar to Pikachu's. Mimikyu lives in dark places because it becomes sick when it is exposed to sunlight, so it is rumored that this is why it wears its covering.[3] Mimikyu is always covered by its disguise, so its actual appearance is unknown. However, glimpses of it have been seen, such as its claw, its underside, and its eyes. It is believed that seeing its true form from underneath the cloth will be afflicted with a mysterious illness, so the people of Alola dare not try to peek.[3] A scholar who saw underneath Mimikyu's rag was so horrified that they died from shock. A Pokémon Trainer who accidentally saw its true form due to a gust of wind and a curious scientist who wanted to know what it looks like were unfortunate enough to encounter a similar fate, with the former dying painfully that night and the latter succumbing to a mysterious illness. Mimikyu will become agitated and violently stop anyone from attempting to look underneath its rag. Until recent times, it was initially thought by humans to be just a ghost wearing a cloth. As mentioned in Pokémon Sleep, Mimikyu mimics Pikachu to the point of even sleeping like it.[4]

Busted Form

When Mimikyu's disguise is damaged, it shifts to its Busted Form. The costume's head droops limply backward, exposing that the Pikachu-like appearance is merely a disguise. If the damage is severe enough to tear the rag, shadowy vapors emerge from the concealed body hidden within the costume.

Mimikyu changes into its Busted Form when it is hit by an attack and its neck breaks, causing it to slump over and no longer convincingly resemble a Pikachu. It is known to feel deep sadness when its true form is revealed, though it is likely that it is not actually in pain despite its broken neck. Ordinarily, the rag it wears allows Mimikyu to avoid attacks. Mimikyu will attempt to keep up its disguise, even if its rag is broken, but if it does get damaged, Mimikyu will spend all night patching it in front of a mirror as if its life depends on it. Repairing the rag correctly is often difficult for Mimikyu, so it cries a lot on the inside. It then seeks revenge on the one that damaged it, fearing that its true identity could have been exposed. Unforgiving, Mimikyu will take down the culprit even at the risk of costing its own life in the process. It will never forget the person who damaged its rag for as long as it is still alive.

During Trainer Battles in Pokémon GO, Mimikyu will change to Busted Form when hit with a charged attack, which it will be protected from taking any damage from. It will remain in its Busted Form for the rest of the battle. While in Busted Form, its Defense drops.

Form data

Mimikyu has two interchangeable forms: Disguised Form and Busted Form. It will always be in its Disguised Form outside of battle. Mimikyu starts battles in its Disguised Form. When it is hit by a damaging move (or hits itself in confusion), its Ability Disguise reduces the damage it takes from it: in Generation VII, it takes no damage from it at all, and in Generation VIII onward, it nullifies the attack's damage but then loses 1/8 of its maximum HP. Afterwards, it changes into Busted Form. While in Busted Form, it can be damaged normally. Mimikyu will stay in Busted Form until either it faints or the battle ends. Mimikyu has a unique form change animation that shows its disguise slumping to the side when it changes to Busted Form.

Although Mimikyu's Disguise Ability cannot be suppressed or replaced, Mimikyu can be damaged normally without changing its form by moves that ignore Abilities (namely Sunsteel Strike, Moongeist Beam, Photon Geyser, Searing Sunraze Smash, Menacing Moonraze Maelstrom, Light That Burns the Sky, G-Max Drum Solo, G-Max Fireball, and G-Max Hydrosnipe) and by Pokémon with Abilities that ignore other Abilities (namely Mold Breaker, Turboblaze, and Teravolt).

Despite Pokémon Legends: Z-A not featuring Abilities, Mimikyu still takes reduced damage and changes form when first attacked in a battle.

Main series

Major appearances

Jessie's Mimikyu

Mimikyu debuted in Loading the Dex!. Though Ash tried to catch it, it decided to side with Team Rocket instead after spotting Pikachu and becoming enraged by his presence. Mimikyu was properly caught by Jessie in First Catch in Alola, Ketchum-Style!.

Other

A Shiny Mimikyu, nicknamed Mimikins (Japanese ミミたん Mimi-tan), debuted in Why Not Give Me a Z-Ring Sometime?, under the ownership of Acerola. Unlike regular Mimikyu, Mimikins is an actual ghost of a deceased Mimikyu, and thus can float in the air and pass through solid objects. It has since made further appearances in Pokémon the Series: Sun & Moon. Although Mimikins is Shiny, part of its disguise is colored like a common Mimikyu, having been patched up to it.

Minor appearances

A Trainer's Mimikyu appeared in Crystal-Clear Sleuthing!.

Other appearances

Super Smash Bros. Ultimate

Mimikyu appears as a Pokémon summoned from a Poké Ball, where it uses its exclusive Z-Move Let's Snuggle Forever[6] on any opponent who gets too close to it. Unlike its depiction in the core series games, Mimikyu grabs its target instead of jumping on top of it. If the target is at 90% damage or more at the end of the attack, they will be instantly KO'd, and Mimikyu will leave behind its disguise and presumably depart afterwards.

Mimikyu also appears as a Spirit. If its Spirit is equipped, the fighter will start the match with Death's Scythe.

Detective Pikachu

A Mimikyu appears during Chapter 6, being a Pokémon that works at the GNN TV studio.

Pokémon UNITE

Main article: Mimikyu (UNITE)

Mimikyu is playable through obtaining a Unite License. It is a melee all-rounder.

Trivia

  • According to an official piece of merchandise, Mimikyu's height does not count the head of the disguise, only the body.[7]
  • Mimikyu is the only Fairy-type Pokémon with a serpentine body.
  • In Pokémon Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon, it is the only obtainable Totem-like Pokémon that is not version exclusive.
    • This makes Mimikyu the only Pokémon with an internal "totem-like form" that can perform a form change. While Totem Wishiwashi can change form, it uses the same forms as other Wishiwashi.
  • In the game data of Pokémon Sun and Moon, Busted Form Mimikyu can learn Surf via TM, despite Disguised Form Mimikyu being unable to. Since Disguised Form Mimikyu is the only form that exists outside of battle, there is no way to teach this move to Mimikyu. Busted Form Mimikyu can no longer learn Surf in Pokémon Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon and subsequent games.
  • Despite Mimikyu having disguise mechanic specifically designed for Trainer Battles, Mimikyu originally couldn't be used there except against Team GO Rocket for over 2 months until after it was added to the game, when the Trainer Battle overhauls was implemented.
  • On October 20, 2016, The Pokémon Company posted a Mimikyu-themed music video onto their YouTube channel titled Mimikyu's Song.

Concept and development

Mimikyu was designed by Megumi Mizutani.[8]

Cultural impact

In the Pokémon of the Year poll held by The Pokémon Company in 2020, Mimikyu was voted the most popular Generation VII Pokémon, receiving 99,077 votes. It was also the third-most popular Pokémon overall.

Glitches

Due to a glitch, if Mimikyu is hit by Rollout or Ice Ball, the damage multiplier for these moves can also be applied to other moves. Mimikyu shares this trait with Eiscue. This glitch was patched out in Version 1.3.1 of Pokémon Sword and Shield.

Origin

Mimikyu's disguise is based on Pikachu, having its crudely drawn "face" resembling a children's drawing made using crayon. The execution of the disguise is similar to a ghost portrayed using bedsheets as a type of costume, often having a face drawn or cut-out. In-universe, Mimikyu's disguise is due to the rising popularity of Pikachu-themed merchandise 20 years prior.[9] This may indirectly reference the Pokémon 20th Anniversary, as Pokémon Sun and Moon were released in celebration of this event. Additionally, it references Pikachu's own status as the Pokémon franchise's mascot and the concept of "Pokémania", the rise of Pokémon's popularity during its global release. This would also suggest that, illustrated by its appearance at the Thrifty Megamart (Abandoned Site), that Mimikyu may also be based on Pikachu dolls. Mimikyu's fake tail appears to be made of wood, as shown by its use of Wood Hammer.

Mimikyu also shares similarities with the Bugul Noz, a Breton myth about a kind and gentle fairy spirit whose horrifying ugliness causes it to be perpetually alone. The fact that Mimikyu's true appearance can kill may be a reference to Lovecraftian or eldritch monsters; if any person were to see their true forms, they would either be driven insane or die.

Name origin

Mimikyu and Mimikkyu may be derived from mimic and きゅうきゅう kyūkyū (onomatopoeia for squeaking, similar to Pikachu). It may also involve a corruption of 迷宮 meikyuu (mystery).
